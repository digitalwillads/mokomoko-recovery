What happened to Google Ads after the rebrand

Mokomoko · Digital Will Ads · October 2, 2026

What happened · 1

On Aug 6, Google stopped every ad for about 2.5 days

At 8 AM Pacific, impressions dropped from about 120 an hour to 2, across all three campaigns at once. The ads themselves had not changed. The stop came as the site moved from akiya-mart.com to mokomoko.com.

What happened · 4

We ruled out the usual suspects

Market pricesGoogle's lowest top-of-page bid is still $0.05 to $0.08. Competition is “Low.”
CompetitorsOnly two small rivals show up, each under 10% of impressions.

PolicyNo disapprovals, strikes or appeals on record.

What happened · 5

Most likely cause: the new domain has no ad history

The market did not get more expensive. Our ads did. Google rates ads partly on the click history of the site they link to. akiya-mart.com had years of history, and mokomoko.com started from zero.

The good news: the price is easing as history builds, from $0.68 in mid August to about $0.35 in late September.

What happened · SEO

Organic search traffic has halved since the move

Google has not yet passed akiya-mart.com's rankings to mokomoko.com. Top-10 rankings for the old domain fell from 543 in July to 334 in September. The new domain holds 3. Traffic was already sliding before the rebrand, so the move made an existing drop worse.
